01 — FRONTAL LOBE

The Frontal Lobe

The frontal lobe is involved in planning, decision-making and problem-solving. It also helps control voluntary movement and plays an important role in personality and social behavior.

Planning Decision-making Problem-solving Motor control
02 — PARIETAL LOBE

The Parietal Lobe

The parietal lobe processes information coming from the senses. It helps the brain understand touch, pressure, temperature and the position of the body in space.

Touch Sensation Spatial awareness Body perception
03 — TEMPORAL LOBE

The Temporal Lobe

The temporal lobe is strongly involved in hearing, memory and language. It also helps the brain recognize and understand information.

Memory Hearing Language Recognition
04 — OCCIPITAL LOBE

The Occipital Lobe

The occipital lobe is mainly responsible for processing visual information. It helps the brain interpret colors, shapes, movement and what we see.

Vision Colors Shapes Visual processing
05 — CEREBELLUM

The Cerebellum

The cerebellum helps coordinate movement, maintain balance and control posture. It is also important for precise and well-coordinated motor skills.

Balance Coordination Movement Motor skills
